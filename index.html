        Good to know

        • November nights are cool, around 18–26 °C. Bring a jacket.
        • Save a trusted tricycle driver's number. Ride-hailing is hard to find, and you'll make several condo trips on Day 2.
        • Agree on tricycle fares before riding, especially at night.
        • Fog rolls in fast. Views are clearest early morning.
        • Traffic builds on Aguinaldo Highway on the way home, so leave buffer time.
        • Check PHIVOLCS for Taal advisories before the trip.
        • Keep ₱500–₱1,000 emergency cash outside the budget.
